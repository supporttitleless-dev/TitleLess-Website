Common questions

Answers, shortest first.

Getting around
Where did the tabs go?

Tap the address capsule: the panel opens with your open pages on it. The ⋯ menu carries Tabs as well. TitleLess keeps them off the screen rather than off the phone — the top of the display is the page, not a strip of thumbnails.

How do I get back to Home?

Tap the address capsule, or open the ⋯ menu. Either brings up the panel with favourites, history, downloads and settings on it. If you would rather have it on a gesture, Settings → App Behaviour → Open Home by swiping down moves it to a downward swipe — and pull-to-refresh takes the capsule's place.

Pages and sites
A page will not load, or a check never finishes

Some sites gate on browser checks that need JavaScript and cookies. Open Settings → Safeguards and make sure Block JavaScript is off, then reload.

A site renders wrongly, or something is missing

Usually one filter rule being too keen. In Settings, turn Block Ads & Trackers down to Off, reload the page, and tell us whether that fixed it. That one line saves us a week.

An ad got through

Send the address of the page. Filter lists update roughly daily, and a rule for it may already be on the way.

Downloads
Where do my downloads go?

Into the app's own Downloads, reachable from the panel. From there you can move a file into Files or share it anywhere.

Privacy and Face ID
Face ID is not offered

Check that iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps lists TitleLess and the switch is on.

How do I clear everything?

Both live in Settings itself, not behind another screen. Clear History & Website Data forgets the pages you visited along with the cookies and caches of every site. Shred App Data goes further and puts the app back exactly as it arrived — pages, history, favourites, downloads list and every setting. Files you already downloaded stay in Files, where they are yours to delete.

What do you collect?

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no server behind TitleLess — the privacy policy spells out exactly what leaves your phone and why.

The look
The glass looks flat, or too busy

Settings → Glass & Wallpaper → Glass Material switches between Frosted and clear glass, and See Through sets how much of the page behind comes through.

Can I use my own wallpaper?

Yes — a photo, or a live video. Settings → Glass & Wallpaper, then Choose Photo or Choose Live Video.
