Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026 · TitleLess for iOS · Developer: Baljinder Singh

TitleLess has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. Nothing you do in it is sent to its developer, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What is stored, and where

History, bookmarks, downloads, saved pages, open pages, wallpapers and your settings are held on your device only. They are never uploaded, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone. Downloaded files are kept encrypted inside the app until you save them to Files. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

A private page keeps its history, cookies and cache in memory alone. Closing the app discards them.

Clear History & Website Data, in Settings, removes the pages you have visited along with the cookies, caches and local storage of every site. Shred App Data removes everything TitleLess has stored — pages, history, bookmarks, favourites, downloads, saved pages, wallpapers and every setting — and puts it back the way it arrived. Downloads you have already saved to Files are left there, where they are yours to delete.

What connects to the network

  • The pages you choose to visit, and whatever those pages themselves request. A page you open is between you and that site; TitleLess does not add anything to it or report it anywhere.
  • Searches go to the search engine you picked in Settings — TitleLess does not attach an identifier.
  • Search suggestions: while you type in the address bar, the text so far is sent to that same search engine to fetch suggestions, over a connection that carries no cookies and no identifier. This never happens in private browsing or for anything that looks like a web address.
  • Site icons are fetched from the site whose page you are on, over a connection that carries no cookies and keeps nothing on disk.
  • Filter lists are downloaded from the addresses shown under Content Filtering, including any you add yourself, and re-checked about once a day. Those requests carry no cookies and say nothing about you beyond the fact that someone asked for a public file.

Permissions

TitleLess asks for the camera, microphone, location or motion only when a website you are using asks for them, and a site gets them only if you allow it. What they capture goes to that site; TitleLess itself keeps and sends none of it. Location, in Settings › Privacy & Safeguards › Privacy, turns location off for every website.

Saving an image from a page asks for permission to add to Photos, and only to add — TitleLess cannot see your library. Choosing a photo or file for a page, or a photo or video for the background, uses the system picker, which hands over only the item you pick.

Opening a page on your own network, such as a router's settings page, asks for local network access the first time.

What is not done

No advertising identifier is read, and contacts are never requested.

No crash reports or usage statistics are collected.

Blocking

Ad and tracker blocking runs entirely on your device. The rules are files; the addresses of the pages you visit are matched against them there, and are never sent anywhere to be checked.

Face ID and Touch ID

Content you keep in the app can be put behind Face ID or Touch ID. That check is performed by iOS, and the app is told only whether it succeeded. No biometric data is ever available to the app.

Children

TitleLess shows the web, which is not a curated place. It is not directed at children and has no content rating of its own.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or third-party scripts. GitHub keeps standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for security and operational purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and material changes are noted in the changelog.
