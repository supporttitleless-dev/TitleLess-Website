Ad blocking

The web, minus the parts that follow you.

Filter lists are matched on your phone against every request a page makes. Nothing about what you browse is sent anywhere to be checked.

  • Intrusive ads removed — banners, interstitials, the video that plays before the video, and the pop-under that opens behind the page.
  • Pages load lighter — a blocked request is never fetched, so there is less to download and less to draw.
  • Less of your data spent — the heaviest thing on most pages is the advertising, and it never arrives.
  • Fewer bad neighbourhoods — malicious ads are a common way in, and the filter lists take most of them out.
  • Cookie notices hidden — you stop dismissing the same banner on every site.
Privacy

Private browsing that is actually private.

A private page keeps its history, cookies and cache in memory alone, and the app discards them when you close it. Every surface goes black while you are in it, so the mode is never in doubt.

  • Browse without being logged — a private page keeps its history and cookies in memory alone, and drops them when you close the app.
  • No tracking, no profile — trackers and fingerprinting are blocked, and nothing about you is built, stored or sold.
  • Locked with your face — private browsing can ask for Face ID before it opens.
  • Safer on public Wi-Fi — HTTPS-Only Mode refuses to load a page in the clear, so the café network sees nothing but the name of the site.
  • Warned off bad sites — known phishing and malware hosts are flagged before the page loads.
  • Shred App Data — one tap puts the app back exactly as it arrived.
Glass & Wallpaper

  • See Through — how much of the page shows through the panels.
  • Wallpaper — gradients, your own photograph, or a live video.

Questions

Before you download.

Is TitleLess free?

Yes. No purchase, no subscription, no account, and no ads of our own.

How can it be private with no server?

That is why it is. Blocking happens on your phone, your history stays in the app, and there is no account to attach it to. The privacy policy lists every request the app makes.

Where did the tabs go?

Tap the address capsule and the panel opens, with your open pages on it alongside favourites, history and downloads. The ⋯ menu has Tabs on it too. They are off the screen, not off the phone — the top of the display belongs to the page.

Does blocking break websites?

Occasionally a rule is too keen. Open Settings and turn Block Ads & Trackers down, then send us the address so it can be fixed for everybody.

Which devices does it run on?

Any iPhone on iOS 26, and any iPad on iPadOS 26 — one download covers both. Version 1.0 remains available from iOS 18.
