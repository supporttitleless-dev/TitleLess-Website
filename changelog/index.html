What's new in TitleLess.

Every release, newest first — what changed and what it means for you.

1.6 In review

Keep what you came for.

Some things are worth having offline — a recipe before the flight, a tutorial on a train. TitleLess now saves them properly, and keeps them where you put them.

  • Pause and pick it up later. Lost signal in a lift? It waits, then carries on where it stopped instead of starting again.
  • Keeps going in the background. Lock the phone, read something else, come back to a finished file.
  • Honest progress. A bar that moves as bytes arrive, not one that sits at 0% and then jumps to done.
  • Behind Face ID, if you like. Anything you save can sit in a locked shelf only you open.

What you may save is between you and the site you take it from — see the terms.

Also in 1.6

1.6
  • Glass, your way. Choose frosted or clear glass, and how much of the page shows through it.
  • Living wallpapers. A video behind the glass, or one of your own photographs — alongside the built-in gradients.
  • Gradient on the glass alone. Colour in the panels while the page stays the page.
  • Reader. The article, without the furniture around it.
  • No more white flash. The strip at the top keeps the page's own colour from launch through loading.
  • Support on the web. Help and the privacy policy now live at titlelessbrowser.com, so they stay current between releases.
  • Requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26. Version 1.0 stays available from iOS 18.

First release

1.0 28 August 2026
  • TitleLess arrives on the App Store: no title bar, one glass capsule, and the page running the full height of the display.
  • Ads, trackers, pop-ups and fingerprinting blocked from the first page you open.
  • Private browsing that keeps nothing, and can sit behind Face ID.
  • Wallpapers, and the settings to shape the glass over them.
